Details & FAQs.

What’s included?

$2,499 for 12 months of agreed website and software work, directly with Leon. Scope and costs are confirmed before you pay. The initial discussion is free.

How do requests work?

Email me. We agree on one task at a time before work. If the scope changes, I pause for approval. Larger jobs can be split or quoted separately.

Full sites or apps?

Projects are scoped individually. Full apps and large websites usually need a separate quote. No unlimited work or guaranteed completion date. A website builder may suit basic DIY edits.

Multiple projects?

Yes. We agree on each task, with one task at a time. Larger projects may need a separate quote.

What happens next?

I reply with a first task, cost, and written terms. You see those before any payment. Discuss by email or a free 15-minute call.

Reply and delivery times?

I aim to reply within two business days, Monday–Friday excluding holidays. Delivery is agreed per task, based on the work and required access or content. No emergency support or delivery guarantee.

Who owns the work?

You own paid custom work and receive the agreed source code and setup notes. Accounts stay in your name. Existing, third-party, and open-source tools keep their licenses. Ownership is confirmed in writing before payment.

Extra costs?

Domains, hosting, email, store plans, payment fees, AI/API usage, and other subscriptions cost extra. Maintenance is agreed per request. Large builds, data migrations, ongoing content entry, security audits, regulated-data systems, and 24/7 support need separate agreements. No extra spend or work without approval.

Cancel or renew?

12 months from the agreed start. Renewal is manual: no automatic charge. Cancel by email. Current/completed months are not refundable; unused future full months are refunded at $208.25 each. Written terms and legal rights apply.
