Operational client system · ALLCPR

ALLCPR Site Intelligence

Problem: ALLCPR needed a consistent way to compare where to open, staff, and grow instead of manually searching maps or trusting one headline score.

Built: Leon built a location-planning system from a reviewed project dataset containing 33,772 U.S. ZIP-code records. It brings together targeted local-business map data, public business and facility data, ALLCPR's history, uncertainty ranges, reasons for and against each area, maps, and Excel exports for the team.

Reliability check: Leon found and removed a misleading validation loop, so the platform shows uncertainty instead of presenting false confidence as fact.

Decision boundary: The platform prioritizes markets to investigate. Rent, parking, classroom fit, instructor coverage, and current local conditions still require human field validation before opening.

Role: data product and full-stack developer · ALLCPR client project

Internal workflow · client anonymized

Review desk

Problem: Review notifications arrived faster than a small team could classify and answer them consistently.

Built: An inbox workflow that classifies the issue, drafts from verified business facts, and routes a Gmail draft, Slack card, and sheet row to a person.

Control: The workflow never posts a reply. A human reviews and sends it.

Internal workflow · client anonymized

Document control

Problem: Company documents and approvals lived across folders, inboxes, and memory.

Built: A template request creates and files the document; approval locks it; publishing increments the version, writes the change log, and exports the external PDF.

Handoff: The workflow was implemented in Apps Script and as six editable n8n workflows.
