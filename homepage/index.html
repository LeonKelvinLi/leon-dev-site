CLEAR FROM DAY ONE

A few good questions.

You should know exactly what you’re getting before we start.

What does the membership include?

24 hours over 12 months, released as two hours at the start of each membership month. Unused hours carry forward within that year and expire when the membership ends. Planning, calls, design, development, testing, revisions, and troubleshooting all use this allowance. The initial fit review is free.

How do requests and estimates work?

Send your ideas directly to Leon by email. We choose one active request at a time. You receive an estimate and a clear deliverable before work starts. You approve the work and its use of your available hours. If an estimate changes, work pauses for your approval. A large idea can be split into smaller steps or quoted separately.

Can you build a whole app for $2,499?

It depends on the scope. A focused website, integration, or small tool may fit; a complex application usually will not. The examples on this page are starting points for a discussion, not promises of a complete product. We confirm fit and the first deliverable before you pay. There is no unlimited work or guaranteed delivery date.

How quickly will you reply or deliver?

I aim to reply within two business days, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. This is a response target, not a delivery guarantee or emergency support service. We agree on scheduling for each request based on its scope, available hours, and the access or content you need to provide.

Who owns the finished work?

You own the custom deliverables you have paid for and receive the agreed source code and setup notes. Your accounts stay in your name. Pre-existing tools and third-party or open-source components keep their original licenses. These details are included in your written agreement.

What costs are separate?

Domains, hosting, email, store platforms, payment processing, AI usage, and other third-party subscriptions are paid by you. Large projects, data migrations, ongoing content entry, security audits, regulated-data systems, and 24/7 support are outside this membership unless separately agreed. No extra spend or work is authorized without your approval.

How do renewal and cancellation work?

The membership lasts 12 months from the agreed start date. Renewal is manual: no automatic charge. You can cancel by email. Completed and current membership months are not refundable; unused future full months are refunded at $208.25 each. Unused hours expire at cancellation. Your written agreement confirms these terms before payment, including any rights required by law.

What happens when my hours run out?

We can wait for next month’s two hours, use hours already carried forward, or agree on a separate quote. Future hours are not available early unless agreed in writing. I share time used and time remaining with each work update, so you can decide what comes next.
