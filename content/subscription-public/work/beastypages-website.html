beastypages.com case study.

A client needed one phone-first website for browsing local-business pages and menus in a consistent interface. Leon built the public site and the supporting demonstration flow while keeping the unfinished commerce boundary explicit.

Role: Website and full-stack developer · Client website project · reviewed September 4, 2026

The decision this solved

Make many business pages feel like one phone-first website.

The website had to organize many distinct businesses without making a phone visitor relearn the interface on every page. Search, business details, menus, and the cart demonstration had to remain understandable in one compact experience.
