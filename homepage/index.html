PRICE, TIME & PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you join.

What’s included, what costs extra, and how we work together.

What does the membership include?

You pay $2,499 once for 12 months. You get two hours at the start of each month: 24 hours over 12 months. Unused hours carry forward within that year and expire when the membership ends. Calls, planning, design, coding, testing, revisions, and troubleshooting all count toward your time. There is no charge for the first discussion to check whether I can help.

How do I ask for changes?

Email me what you need. We choose one task at a time. I explain what I’ll do and how many hours I expect it to take. You approve that before I start. If I find it will take longer, I pause and ask you before using more time. Bigger jobs can be broken into smaller steps or quoted separately.

Can you build a whole app for $2,499?

This membership pays for an agreed amount of my time. A small website or software job may fit; a complete app or large website usually needs a separate quote. I’ll tell you what I can realistically do before you pay. The examples on this page are jobs to discuss, not complete products included at a fixed price. Work is not unlimited, and there is no guaranteed completion date.

Can I bring more than one project?

Yes. You can ask for work on different projects over the year. We work on one agreed task at a time, and every project uses the same hours. Calls, revisions, and troubleshooting count too. For a bigger job, we may need to save up unused hours or agree on a separate quote.

What can two hours cover?

A page update, a broken form, or a small change to existing software may fit. It depends on how your site is built and what access is available, so I check before estimating. These are not fixed two-hour packages. You start with two hours, then get another two each month. The full 24 hours are not available on day one.

What happens after I send a request?

I read it and ask any questions I need to understand the job. If I can help, I send a description of the first task, an estimate in hours, and the written terms. You see those before any payment. If the job needs a separate project quote, I’ll explain that. We can talk by email or on a free 15-minute call.

How quickly will you reply or deliver?

I aim to reply within two business days, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. That is a reply target, not a promise to finish the job in two days. We agree on timing for each task based on the work, the hours you have available, and any access or content I need from you. Emergency support is not included.

Who owns the finished work?

You own the custom work you’ve paid for, and you receive the source code and setup notes agreed in writing. Your accounts stay in your name. Existing tools, third-party software, and open-source components keep their original licenses. Your written agreement explains these details before payment.

What costs are separate?

You pay separately for your web address, hosting, email, store plans, payment fees, and paid services such as AI tools or APIs. Agreed maintenance and updates use your hours. Large projects, moving large amounts of data, ongoing content entry, security audits, systems handling regulated data, and 24/7 support need a separate agreement. I don’t add paid services or extra work without your approval.

How do renewal and cancellation work?

The membership lasts 12 months from the agreed start date. Renewal is manual: no automatic charge. You can cancel by email. Completed and current membership months are not refundable; unused future full months are refunded at $208.25 each. Unused hours expire at cancellation. Your written agreement confirms these terms before payment, including any rights required by law.

What happens when my hours run out?

We can wait for next month’s two hours, use unused time you’ve already saved, or agree on a separate quote. Future hours are not available early unless agreed in writing. Each work update shows the time used and the time left, so you can decide what to do next.
